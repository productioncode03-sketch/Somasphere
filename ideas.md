# Somasphere Design Direction

## Three Initial Approaches

### Theme Name: Learning Field Notes
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, editorial learning environment inspired by Kenyan field notebooks, classroom wall charts, and well-used study guides. It feels grounded, human, and quietly optimistic.
**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Savannah Signal
**Very Brief Intro:** A high-contrast digital learning system using deep earth tones, bright signal colors, and strong spatial hierarchy. It gives learners momentum without leaning into generic neon or gaming tropes.
**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Chalk & Circuit
**Very Brief Intro:** A tactile blend of chalkboard texture, crisp academic typography, and lightweight digital cards. It makes revision feel familiar while keeping the product modern.
**Probability:** 0.03

## Selected Approach: Learning Field Notes

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design with East African educational poster references, material warmth, and restrained Swiss-style information hierarchy.

### Core Principles
1. Make progress visible through clear, calm information architecture.
2. Combine tactile learning cues with precise digital structure.
3. Use asymmetry and generous whitespace to reduce cognitive load.
4. Treat every interaction as a small moment of encouragement, not pressure.

### Color Philosophy
The palette is anchored in paper cream, ink charcoal, and a signature acacia green. Terracotta marks active learning and warm sun yellow marks moments of discovery. The colors should feel rooted in place and daylight rather than synthetic or overly saturated.

### Layout Paradigm
Pages use an editorial split composition: a strong left rail or column establishes context, while the main content unfolds in offset blocks. Dashboards should feel like an annotated study desk rather than a dense admin grid.

### Signature Elements
- Fine notebook-rule lines and subtle paper grain.
- Small chapter labels and hand-marked progress ticks.
- Acacia-leaf geometry used sparingly as a brand motif.

### Interaction Philosophy
Controls should respond with short, tactile transitions: a slight lift, a crisp color shift, and clear focus states. Empty states should teach the next action. Errors should sound practical and kind.

### Animation
Use 160–240ms ease-out transitions for buttons, tabs, and navigation. Stagger page sections by 40–60ms. Use gentle opacity and translate transitions only; never animate layout dimensions. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System
Use Fraunces for display headings and section titles, paired with Source Sans 3 for body copy, labels, and controls. Headlines should be compact and characterful; body copy should remain highly legible at mobile sizes. Use uppercase micro-labels with tracking for chapter metadata.

### Brand Essence
Somasphere is a focused study companion for Kenyan CBE learners who want to understand, practise, and keep moving forward—with tools shaped around their curriculum and context.

Personality: grounded, encouraging, purposeful.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and hopeful. CTAs are specific and action-oriented. Microcopy acknowledges effort without sounding childish or generic.

Example lines:
- “Turn today’s topic into tomorrow’s confidence.”
- “Pick up where your thinking left off.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact circular mark built from three interlocking study arcs, suggesting a sphere, a learning cycle, and a shared classroom. The wordmark should use a custom-modified serif treatment rather than a default text logo.

### Signature Brand Color
Acacia Green — `#2F6B4F` — a confident, ownable green that signals growth, focus, and grounded progress.

## Style Decisions

- The wordmark uses a deliberately modified serif lockup with a colored initial and a small terracotta annotation dot; the navigation mark uses the generated learning-cycle logo.
- The global page texture includes notebook rules and grain so the field-note materiality remains present beyond the landing illustration.
- Future cards should continue moving toward study slips, workbook panels, and annotated chart objects rather than generic SaaS containers.
- Kenyan CBE context should appear through pathway labels, practice language, and study artifacts on every major route.
